## Stance

This is not a results talk, and not a claim that the three problems are new.  It is an invitation to collaborate:

> Bhaiyya log, thoda dekho zara: these are real industry problems, some of them not even cleanly defined, and they need smart people looking at them.

The talk introduces a bridge between two worlds.  Industry has consequential problems, systems, constraints, and partial approaches; academia has the language and tools to formulate and solve them.  The speaker's contribution is to expose the gap honestly, connect it to existing theory, and offer precise starting points—not to claim completed results or preserve credit.

Each problem could fill its own talk once mature.  This talk should therefore:

- make one clean formulation or candidate formulation visible;
- distinguish settled theory from the genuinely unresolved industrial remainder;
- show enough implementation reality to explain why the remainder matters;
- end with questions and invitations, not claims of completion.

The opening disclaimer should convey this stance briefly and warmly.  The quoted Hinglish is the private tonal reference, not necessarily the final slide copy.
