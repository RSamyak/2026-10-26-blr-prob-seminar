# Audience: Bangalore Probability Seminar

## The seminar

- Regular probability seminar in Bengaluru since 2016; meets about twice a month, usually Mondays.
- Rotates across ISI Bangalore, IISc Mathematics, ICTS-TIFR, and TIFR-CAM.  Hybrid: talks also go out on Zoom.
- Organisers named across listings: D Yogeshwaran, Sreekar Vadlamani, Siva Athreya, Lakshmi Priya, and Koushik Ramachandran.
- researchseminars.org lists the audience as "Advanced learners".
- Slots seen in 2026 are about 50 minutes each (for example, 2:00–2:50 and 3:00–3:50 on 5 October).

## Who is in the room

- Faculty, postdocs, and PhD students in probability: ISI (Siva Athreya, Parthanil Roy, D Yogeshwaran, and Mathew Joseph), IISc (Manjunath Krishnapur, Arvind Ayyer, Srikanth Iyer, Sanchayan Sen, Rajesh Sundaresan, Aditya Gopalan, Himanshu Tyagi, and Gugan Thoppe), ICTS and TIFR-CAM (Riddhipratim Basu, Anirban Basak, Nishant Chandgotia, Koushik Ramachandran, and Sreekar Vadlamani), and IIM (Rishideep Roy).
- Some of them work in statistics, learning, or networks: Aditya Gopalan (RL and bandits), Himanshu Tyagi (information theory), Rajesh Sundaresan (sero-survey design), and Gugan Thoppe (stochastic approximation).

## What they usually hear

- Mostly pure probability: interacting particle systems, ASEP and TASEP, random geodesics, Gaussian zeros, random graphs, and Markov chain cutoff.
- Occasional statistics or ML: Vladimir Vovk on martingales, e-values, and conformal testing (2021); Amit Deshpande (MSR) on fair ML (August 2026); and Aditya Gopalan on RL.

## Implications

- Martingales, optional stopping, concentration, and measure theory are home ground.  Do not over-explain them.
- They know sequential testing and e-values exist, and some know them well.  Expect sharp questions on anytime-valid inference.
- They know little about business: metrics, revenue, product teams, why 1% matters, and how platforms are built.  The intro section carries this.
- They want precise formulations and open problems they could work on.  Hand-waving will be noticed.
- Speaker: BMath ISI Bangalore 2018 and PhD Stanford Statistics 2023, so it is partly a home crowd.
